About V Meal

The campus food ordering platform built exclusively for VIT students and faculty.

V Meal is a campus-focused food ordering platform designed exclusively for university students and faculty. Our goal is to simplify the process of ordering food from nearby restaurants while ensuring convenience, transparency, and efficiency within the campus environment.

By connecting students with trusted local eateries, V Meal allows users to browse menus, view nutritional information, place orders, and track their status in real-time. The platform is tailored to address real campus constraints, offering a structured and organized alternative to generic food delivery services.

V Meal aims to enhance the student dining experience by combining technology, accessibility, and thoughtful design — making food ordering smarter, faster, and more reliable for university communities.
